Ola Sayulita Transportation

Ola Transportation in Sayulita provides all of your transportation needs! Locally owned and with over 15 years experience, we will take you where you want to go in style.

We would like to inform you of our new January 2024 pricing,

One-way fee up to 7 people: $2,500 pesos (Airport - Sayulita)

One-way fee 8 - 10 people: $3,000 pesos (Airport - Sayulita)

One-way fee 8 - 12 people: $3,400 pesos (Airport - Sayulita)

One-way fee up to 7 people: $1,800 pesos (Sayulita - Airport)

One-way fee 8 - 10 people: $2,600 pesos (Sayulita - Airport)

One-way fee 8 - 12 people: $3,000 pesos (Sayulita - Airport)

Round trip special for 1- 3 people: $3,500 pesos (Airport - Sayulita, Sayulita - Airport)

Round trip special 4 - 7 people: $4,000 pesos (Airport - Sayulita, Sayulita - Airport)

Round trip special 8 - 10 people: $5,200 pesos (Airport - Sayulita, Sayulita - Airport)

Round trip special 8 - 12 people: $6,200 pesos (Airport - Sayulita, Sayulita - Airport)

*We accept USD based on the daily exchange rate*

These new fees are based on a study that will help us compete with other companies, help us stay on the level of the economic inflations, and help us obtain new van units to give you the transportation quality you deserve.

Thank you on behalf of Sayulita's Transportation companies.

Ola Sayulita Transportation provides comfortable and clean Suburban services in the Sayulita area. Our friendly and reliable drivers will assist you with your luggage and have cold beer and drinks ready for you upon arrival. Our drivers will gladly stop at the super market on the way to Sayulita so you can stock up on groceries.

Reservations can be made with credit cards or PayPal

We understand the stress that can come with traveling, professional drivers always show up early to make sure that you will reach your destination on time. We pride ourselves on our excellent service.

Explore the local area or take a customized tour to such places like:

  • San Pancho
  • Punta Mita
  • San Sabastian
  • The town of Tequila
  • San Blas
  • La Peñita market
  • Puerto Vallarta
  • Mismaloya

Please use the contact form or call the number above (please mention Sayulita Life) to reserve Ola Sayulita!

About Ernesto & Fidel
Ernesto was born here in Sayulita and lived half my life in the united states, until my family and I returned in 2004. As the years progressed and Sayulita became more of a tourist destination I started working in vacation home property management in 2011. This is how I saw the need to have a trustworthy transportation company to fill the needs of our guests.

Fidel is a local from San Pancho that has been working in the transportation business for 15 years. With his experience and knowledge of the area and having worked with various known companies in the state over the years , we decided to create the Nayarit Experience Transportation.
