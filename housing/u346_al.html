Overview

Located in dynamic Somerville, Union 346 offers its residents a perfect blend of modern living and urban convenience. With easy access to the new MBTA Green Line extension, commuting to Harvard and Kendall Squares, or downtown Boston is seamless. The property's amenities are second to none, featuring a rooftop deck with picturesque views of the Boston skyline that residents rave about. Enjoy the vibrant local lifestyle with abundant parks, restaurants, and entertainment options just a short bike ride away. Residents highlight the convenience of an on-site fitness center, pet amenities, and beautiful outdoor spaces perfect for socializing. This truly smoke-free and dog-friendly community perfectly caters to those looking for quality living in an active environment.
