    Rent this Paddleboard for $50 per hour in Nuevo Corral del Risco, Punta de Mita, Mexico. Punta de Mita is an ideal place for surfing, with a great number of excellent locations along the bay. Ideal for beginners and expert surfers. For experts, locations are La Bahía, La Lancha, Punta del Burro and El Faro. For beginners, El Anclote beach. We also offer surf lessons. In addition, aside from surfing, we also offers Snorkeling Trips, Whale Watching, Scuba diving & fishing with our boats available. Surfing Prices $100.00 US for 2 hours 1-8 people Other Trips/Tours Snorkeling There is a beach, caves and a rock structure with the shape of an elephant. Prices $150.00 US for 2 hours 1-8 people $3.00 US each extra person for bracelet Whale Watching Migrating from the north cold winter, the humpback whale find its way to Bahía de Banderas (Bay of Flags) which is considered one of the largest and most beautiful bays in the world. The calid warm waters of the bay are perfect for whales to mate and give birth. The courtship grouping, bird process and nursering are some of the whale behaviours you witness on a whale watching tour.
